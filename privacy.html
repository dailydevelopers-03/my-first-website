Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2026

Information We Collect

Voice Recorder is designed to help users record, trim, organize, and manage audio recordings. We do not require users to create an account and we do not collect personal information unless voluntarily provided by the user.

Permissions Used

The application may request microphone access to record audio. Storage access may also be required to save and manage recordings on your device.

Audio Recordings

Audio recordings created using the application are stored on the user's device. The application does not upload recordings to our servers.

Data Storage

All recordings and related files are stored locally on your device unless you choose to export or share them.

Third-Party Services

The application may use third-party services such as analytics, crash reporting, advertising, or app distribution platforms. These services may collect information according to their own privacy policies.

Internet Access

The application may use an internet connection to display advertisements and support app-related services. Audio recordings created by the user are stored locally on the device and are not uploaded to our servers.

Children's Privacy

The application is not directed toward children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes To This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Changes will be reflected on this page with an updated revision date.

Contact Information

If you have questions regarding this privacy policy, please contact us using the information provided on the Contact page.
